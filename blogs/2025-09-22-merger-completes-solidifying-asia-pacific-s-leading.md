SINGAPORE, 22 September, 2025 — Neuron Mobility and Beam Mobility have completed their merger, bringing together two Singapore-headquartered micromobility leaders. The combined company, Neuron Mobility Holdings, forms one of the world’s top shared e-scooter operators and the clear market leader in the Asia-Pacific region.
